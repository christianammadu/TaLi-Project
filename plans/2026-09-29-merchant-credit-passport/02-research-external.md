## Fintech & MFB Underwriting Standards in Nigeria
- Lenders (Moniepoint, FairMoney, Carbon, Renmoney) evaluate:
  1. Average Daily Turnover & 90-day Revenue Consistency.
  2. Operating Margin & Gross Profit vs Expenses.
  3. Cash Flow Volatility (standard deviation of daily inflows).
  4. Debt-to-Revenue Ratio.
- **Verification Integrity**: Bank officers require independent verification. A QR code pointing to an immutable verification URL validates that the PDF was generated from genuine ledger records.
