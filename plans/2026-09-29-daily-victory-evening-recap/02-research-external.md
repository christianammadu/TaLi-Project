## Retention Mechanics in MSME Fintech
- Timing: 8:00 PM - 9:00 PM is peak reconciliation time when traders close shops and review physical cash boxes.
- Key Metrics to Surface:
  1. Total Gross Sales (and % change vs last week same day).
  2. Cash Collected vs Credit Sales.
  3. Star Product of the Day.
  4. Debts Collected vs New Debts Incurred.
- Free Push on Telegram: Telegram Bot API allows unlimited free push notifications without window restrictions.
