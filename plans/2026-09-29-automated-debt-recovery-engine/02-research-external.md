## Debt Psychology in African Retail
- Confrontational reminders damage customer loyalty. Effective reminders are courteous, cite the exact date and items, and make payment effortless by including account details.
- **Tone options**: Formal Corporate, Friendly Community, or Gentle Pidgin (*'Good day Chief! Gentle reminder of balance...'*).
- **Dynamic Bank Details**: Including NUBAN account number, bank name, and account title reduces payment friction by over 60%.
