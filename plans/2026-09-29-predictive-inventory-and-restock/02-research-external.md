## Retail Inventory Forecasting for FMCG
- Days of Inventory Remaining: `Current Quantity / Average Daily Sales Velocity`.
- Lead Time Buffering: Typical supplier lead times in Nigerian commerce range from 1 to 3 days. Alerts must trigger at `Lead Time + Safety Margin`.
- Proactive Supplier Reorder: Storing the last purchase price and supplier contact allows generating an instant reorder purchase order.
