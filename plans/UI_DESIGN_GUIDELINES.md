# UI & Visual Design Standards for TaLi Plans

This standard applies to all frontend interfaces across TaLi, including the Merchant Web Portal, Marketing Site, Help Center, Telegram Mini App, Digital Receipts, and Credit Dossiers.

## 1. Required Design Skills
When designing, implementing, or polishing any user interface:
- **`impeccable` skill**: Must be invoked for UX architecture, visual hierarchy, spacing systems, typography, responsive behavior, and accessibility.
- **`huashu-design` skill**: Must be invoked for high-fidelity component prototyping, aesthetic polish, micro-interactions, and visual quality gates.

## 2. Prohibited Elements (Zero Tolerance)
- **Emojis are strictly prohibited**:
  - Do NOT use emojis (e.g., 🚀, 🔥, 💰, 📊, 🤖, 👋) anywhere in UI copy, buttons, headers, cards, modals, empty states, notifications, or navigation.
  - Use clean, professional SVG icons instead (e.g., Lucide or Heroicons).
- **Em dashes are strictly prohibited**:
  - Do NOT use em dashes (`—`) anywhere in UI copy, subheadings, feature lists, or marketing prose.
  - Use standard hyphens (`-`), colons (`:`), or cleanly structured sentences.

## 3. Permitted & Recommended Visual Assets
- **Real Photography & Merchant Media**: Authentic photos of market commerce, shops, POS terminals, and realistic device screenshots.
- **Vector Illustrations**: Clean, minimalist vector illustrations (e.g., from unDraw at `undraw.co`).
- **AI-Generated Imagery**: High-fidelity AI-generated visuals (via `generate_image`) for hero art, conceptual merchant workflows, and banner assets.
